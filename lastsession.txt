    words[640];}` (ONE struct level) compiles to THREE nested types:
      _arr_uint_int_640 = OpTypeArray uint 640 (stride 4)
      _Array_natural_uint640 = OpTypeStruct{_arr_uint_int_640} (stride 2560)
      KernelDataBlob_natural = OpTypeStruct{_Array_natural_uint640} (stride 2560)
    View (a) inline dynamic `params->data.words[i]` ends in
      `OpAccessChain %81(_arr_uint_int_640*) %66(i)`  -> runtime WRONG.
    Views (c) base-ptr and (b) raw end in
      `OpPtrAccessChain %147(uint*) %i` (flat scalar pointer) -> runtime CORRECT.
    => Dynamic OpAccessChain on a nested-array-in-struct reached through a
       device pointer miscompiles; OpPtrAccessChain on a flat uint* works.
