STAGE A ITEM 4 — DECISION (USER): both approaches work on the target (RX 570).
 (a) TRUE NARROW STORE: simplest (p16[t]=v), fastest (1 store), RX570-only (SwiftShader

     fallback (keeps SwiftShader runnable).
