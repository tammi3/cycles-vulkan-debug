*** CRITICAL DISCOVERY (part 1 of this session; RESOLVED by steps A-C) ***
`cast<T[]>` / `cast<T*>` are UNAVAILABLE in slangc 2026.17-25-gc7954ebd1
(error[E30015]: undefined identifier 'cast'). bvh2.slang / path_trace.slang
use cast<> and DO NOT COMPILE STANDALONE with this toolchain — treat them as
STALE, not a working reference. There is NO uint64->typed-pointer conversion
either: functional `T*(addr)` parses as multiplication (E39999); `T*(addr)`
and reinterpret forms fail. Only the REVERSE works: `uint64_t(typed_ptr)`
compiles (recover a raw address FROM a typed pointer).

=> RESOLVED (step A of this session): the SoA members in vulkan_data.slang
   were stored as `uint64_t`, but to INDEX them as arrays they MUST be TYPED
   device pointers (uint/float3/float/int/uint16_t/uint8_t POINTERS). A typed
   pointer is 8 bytes = uint64_t, so byte layout and ALL ABI-probe offsets are
   UNCHANGED (re-verified: ABI probe re-run after the retype = ALL MATCH).
