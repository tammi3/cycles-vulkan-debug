VERIFIED FROM AMD SOURCE (not memory):
- EASU (Edge Adaptive Spatial Upsampling): Input = low-res color ONLY (no depth, no normals). 5×5 neighborhood, luminance-based edge detection, 4 directional gradients, 12-tap weighted reconstruction. Output = 2× resolution.
- RCAS (Robust Contrast Adaptive Sharpening): Input = upscaled color. 3×3 neighborhood, local contrast (max-min), 5-tap plus-shaped sharpening kernel (center + 4 diagonals). Optional: CAS pass.
NOT in FSR1: Depth/normal inputs, temporal accumulation, motion vectors. Those are FSR2.
